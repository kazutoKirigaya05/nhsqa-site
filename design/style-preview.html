Three directions for the NHSQA brand

Each one starts from the grey and yellow bell-curve bars on the kit box. They show the same content so you can compare them fairly: the top of the home page, the colors and type, and how the Quant Pipeline map would look. Pick one, or mix parts of two.

A. Trading DeskB. Kit BoxC. Graph Paper

A. Trading Desk

Dark navy with the yellow as a signal color. Feels like the screens quants work on: serious, grown-up, credible to sponsor firms. The pipeline reads like a price chart climbing.

B. Kit Box

The cardboard of the kit becomes the background of the site. Chunky slab type and game-piece shapes; the pipeline is a board-game track. Friendliest of the three and the best fit for younger students.

C. Graph Paper

White graph paper, blue ink, yellow highlighter. Feels like a math notebook: bright, clear and school-friendly. The pipeline is a curve plotted on the grid.
